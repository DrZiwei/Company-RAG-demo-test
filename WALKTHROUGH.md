# Novastone business-review walkthrough

Audience: company decision makers. Company and all evidence are fictional. Snapshot: 2 October 2026. First demo: business review using live AI with labelled replay examples; clients access live mode with a demo code. English is the first interface language.

## Story

Novastone supplies research-use bioscience kits and analytical services. Revenue is growing while delivery, margin, and repeat purchasing weaken. A leader needs to understand what the records support, what remains uncertain, who can decide, and which improvement fits current capacity.

## Six-minute guided demo

1. **Open the knowledge library.** Show 20 documents: financial reports, daily notes, an org chart, customer feedback, supplier memos, quality status, and action/decision records. Point out approved, current, draft, and superseded status. Synthetic audience labels do not enforce access control.
2. **Ask what changed in Q3.** Answer with £2.64m recognised revenue (+10%), £240,000 below target, gross margin 57% versus 61%, on-time shipments 84% versus 94%, reorder rate 72% versus 82%, and turnaround 12 versus 8 working days. Open NVS-003 section citations.
3. **Ask what might explain the changes.** Retrieve the operations delay categories and eight-customer listening memo. Explain plausible links to delivery predictability, distinguish observation from confirmed cause, and show the open Quality investigation.
4. **Ask what leadership should prioritise.** Compare three options and cite the six-specialist-week resource limit. Present the four-week release-record proposal plus two weeks of measurement/coordination as a feasible recommendation. Explain why this cannot include a full supplier project or portal discovery at the same time. Alternative recommendations are acceptable if they respect evidence, capacity, and approval rules.
5. **Review a proposed decision.** Proposed owner Owen; approver Daniel after Priya confirms budget; independent Quality gate for Aisha; £18,000 estimate; suggested review 30 October. The UI shows unresolved conditions and that no project is approved yet. A demo human may approve the simulated project subject to those conditions, or request more evidence. Approval must not be presented as product-release authority or a real payment.
6. **Share and follow up.** Store the question, supporting sections, considered options, chosen response, approver, conditions, and follow-up in a simulated shared decision log. Keep this new log separate from the imported baseline sources so replay examples do not rewrite history.

## Deliberate evidence challenges

- The archived forecast says £2.88m; approved actual revenue is £2.64m.
- Reorder rate is not customer churn, and the quarterly cohorts differ.
- Three lots and four late orders use different units and are not interchangeable.
- An open CAPA and a draft proposal do not prove a confirmed root cause or a validated fix.
- Meeting discussion and task assignment do not constitute project or spending approval.
- Net profit, exact intervention ROI, and an approved Q4 forecast are absent.

## Replay and live behaviour

Provide labelled replay answers for the evaluation questions. Live answers must cite source sections or corresponding PDF pages, expose uncertainty, and distinguish facts from recommendations. Replay is an explicitly saved example rather than an unlabelled substitute for a failed live call. Use a budget/usage-limited server-side AI connection; provider setup and hosted architecture are the next implementation specification.
